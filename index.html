Frequently Asked Questions

How many driving lessons do I need before my road test?

It depends on your experience. Many beginners choose a package of ten lessons, while experienced drivers often start with five. Your instructor will tell you honestly when you are ready, and you can add practice sessions or a warm-up lesson before the exam.

Do I have to take the five hour pre-licensing class?

Yes. New York requires every new driver to complete the five hour class before scheduling a road test. Our class is held on Wednesdays at 5 pm over Zoom. You need to pre-register by phone, and the fee is $60.

Do you offer free pick-up and drop-off?

Yes. We offer free home pick-up and drop-off for students in Brooklyn and Staten Island. Tell us your address and preferred time when you book, and your instructor will plan the lesson around it.

Can I use your car for the road test?

Yes. A car for the road test costs $200, and the road test appointment costs $30. Staten Island tolls are not included. We also recommend a warm-up lesson before the exam, which is included in our packages. Please call to confirm current prices.

Do you teach in Russian?

Yes. Our team is bilingual and works with students in both English and Russian. Our website is also available in Russian for families who prefer it.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash and credit cards. Our packages are listed as cash deals, so please ask about the current options when you call.

Do you offer lessons for nervous drivers?

Absolutely. Many of our students start out afraid of driving. Our instructors are patient, explain every step clearly and let you move at your own pace until you feel comfortable behind the wheel.

Where are you located and what are your hours?

We are at 2246 65th St, Brooklyn, NY 11204. We are open Monday and Tuesday from 10 am to 5 pm, Wednesday from 10 am to 10 pm, and Thursday and Friday from 10 am to 5 pm. Lesson times can be flexible, including weekends by arrangement.

How do I book a driving lesson?
